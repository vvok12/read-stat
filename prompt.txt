Build a desktop app for Linux and MacOS with dotnet & Avalonia. Use MVVM Community Toolkit and SQLite. Apply SukiUI theme.
The app provides gamification of the reading process: 

1. The user is allowed to create cards with: book photo, book title, pages amount, completion progress bar or review stars
2. The user could update the count of pages they have read until the book is done.
3. When the book is completed, there should be an ability to rate it (1,2,3,4, or 5 stars)

There should be 
1. a view to display a list of book cards in historical order (ordered by recency)
2. a view to create/update a card
3. a view to show: total books read, total pages read, count of pages read in this month,
